# TrustNav: oral examination deck

Ten slides for the oral examination (Group 2, 13 November 2026), with speaker notes.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `TrustNav_oral_deck.pptx` | the deck, with the speaker notes on every slide (PowerPoint: View, Notes page) |
| `TrustNav_oral_deck.pdf` | the same ten slides as a PDF, for a room without PowerPoint |
| `TrustNav_speaker_notes.pdf` | printable booklet: running order and timing, one page per slide with its script, and likely questions with short answers |

Every number on the slides is taken from the paper (`paper/trustnav.pdf`) and from the data the engine
exports (`dashboard/public/data/meta.json`, `paper/figures/analysis.json`, `paper/figures/realcheck.json`).
The timeline and sensitivity figures are the paper's; the track of the real drive is drawn from the public
recording described in `engine/trustnav_engine/data/real/README.md`.

Running order: 1 title, 2 problem, 3 system, 4 the three witnesses and the fusion, 5 response layer,
6 evaluation protocol and detection results, 7 second-by-second timelines, 8 the real receiver check,
9 live demo plan, 10 limits, ethics, next steps. About fifteen minutes, then questions.
