**How the team works.** One repository on GitHub with every change reviewed by the lead; the live demo is redeployed automatically on every change; three tutor meetings (the first in the last week of October, the second and third before the oral examination on 13 November 2026). Real data entered the project through public recordings of a real receiver and of real attacks, and through the phone page; a receiver of our own is the next step.
